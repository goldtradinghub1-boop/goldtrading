To get access, you need to create an account through our partner broker's link and meet the eligibility conditions. For more details, contact us on Telegram at @CryptoWhale88_8 or join our free group.
How many trading signals do you provide daily?
Depending on the opportunities identified in the gold market (XAUUSD), we typically provide between 2 and 5 signals per day. Each signal includes clear Entry, Stop Loss (SL) and Take Profit (TP) levels. The number can vary with market conditions — we prioritize the quality of opportunities, not quantity.
Do you have access to my money or profit from my losses?
No. We don't have access to your funds and we don't manage your trading account. The money stays in your personal account, and you keep full control over deposits, withdrawals and trades. We don't receive the money you lose on trades — our collaboration is based on commissions generated through the partner broker.
How does Gold Trading HUB make money if Premium access is free?
Our business model is based on broker partnerships. When you trade through our partner broker, it shares part of the commissions from your trading activity with us, per the affiliate agreement. These commissions are part of the trading costs already applied to your account — we never charge you an extra subscription. Our goal is to build a community of active, disciplined, long-term sustainable traders who trade responsibly and get the best possible results.
